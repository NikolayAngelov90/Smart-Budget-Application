## 5. Key decisions made in this retro

- **Average semantics DECIDED: switch every "3-month average" from mean-over-months-present to a fixed ÷3 window.** Rationale: months-present inflates projections after a one-off spike month (14-4 review finding); a fixed divisor is honest for the simulator and, critically, for Epic 15's Budget Score. Trade-off accepted: new users with <3 months of history see conservative averages. Scope: what-if route, nudge helper (`evaluateNudgeForTransaction`), `forecastEngine`, and any other consumer of month-bucket means — change ONCE, consistently, with tests.
