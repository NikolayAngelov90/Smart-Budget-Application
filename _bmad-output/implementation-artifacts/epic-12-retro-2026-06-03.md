## What Went Well

### 1. 100% Delivery, Every FR Covered
All 8 stories delivered; all 7 epic FRs (FR1–4, FR6, FR8, FR39) satisfied. Every MEDIUM finding fixed before `done`. Clean exit.

### 2. Pure-Engine Architecture Compounded
Seven pure `src/lib/ai/*.ts` (and `src/lib/analytics/*.ts`) engines — `patternDetection`, `forecastEngine`, `nudgeEngine`, `recoveryPlanner`, `seasonalAnalysis`, `reengagementAnalysis`, `aggregateAnalytics` — each paired with a thin route + SWR hook + progressive-disclosure component. Predictable shape eight times over; +173 tests came cheap because the compute layer has no Supabase/Next.js dependencies.

Story 12-8 built the analytics dashboard carried unaddressed since Epic 9/10. Epic 11's AI-4 ("formal cut/commit decision") → committed and shipped, with role-gated access (`analytics_viewer`) and PII-free aggregation.

### 5. Forward De-risking

### 6. Integrity Under Pressure

### 7. Zero Net Debt, Zero Incidents
Continuous Vercel deployment; no rollbacks; TS + ESLint clean throughout.

## What Didn't Go Well

### 1. Hardcoded Currency Recurred — Third Epic Running
Story 12-6 carried a hardcoded `$` in `recommended_action` (MED). This bug class was HIGH in 11.2, HIGH again in 11.7, now MED in 12-6.

**Root cause:** Epic 11's #1 action item (**AI-1: ESLint rule to catch hardcoded currency**) was never implemented. Documentation without enforcement does not prevent recurrence — the exact lesson written in the last retro.

### 2. FR39 Disclaimers Nearly Shipped Missing (HIGH)
Stories 12-1/12-2/12-3 put live AI content in front of users with no financial disclaimer. Epic 11's retro explicitly recommended moving disclaimers to position 12.1 ("legally critical, should not be deferred"). The sprint sequenced it as 12-7 anyway. Caught only by the implementation-readiness assessment (HIGH-1) and patched out-of-band as Story 12-7.

### 3. Plan ↔ Sprint Numbering Drift
Planning Story 12.x ≠ sprint 12-x. Required a mapping table in `epics.md` to preserve traceability. Friction on every cross-reference.

### 4. "Budget" Is a Historical-Average Proxy
No budget-limits table exists. Stories 12-2 ("at risk") and 12-3 ("approaching limit") both use the 3-month historical average as the soft target. Acceptable for MVP, but a conceptual approximation now embedded in user-facing language.

### 5. Uneven Early Review Rigor
Stories 12-1 and 12-2 carry only completion notes (no formal senior-review section); 12-2 left ESLint "pending." Review discipline became consistent from 12-4 onward.
